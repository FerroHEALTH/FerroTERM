- Breaking: in ICD-11 MMS and ICF, a full entity URI is no longer a code, in either its unversioned or versioned form, alone or in an expression. A client that sent `http://id.who.int/icd/release/11/mms/257068234` as `Coding.code` sends the entity id `257068234`. The `$lookup` property `id`, the entity URI, is removed; derive the URI from the system and the id. The Foundation keeps the entity URI as its code.
