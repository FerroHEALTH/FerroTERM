- Breaking: the ICD-11 `$lookup` properties `postcoordinationScale`, `postcoordinationValues`, and the `stem` subproperties `stemLabel` and `stemUri` are removed. An expression's axes and values are the new `postcoordination` properties, and its root is the `stem` Coding, which carries the title. A concept that is no expression no longer lists its axes; its scales stay available as the implicit value sets `<entity uri>/postcoordinationScale/<axis>`.
