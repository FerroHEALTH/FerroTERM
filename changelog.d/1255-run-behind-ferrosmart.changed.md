- FerroTERM moves to `ferrohealth-smart` 0.34, `ferrohealth-session` 0.29, `ferrohealth-telemetry` 0.5.1 and `ferrobridge-term` 0.18, and reads every caller from the token's standard claims. `[auth.issuer.<name>]` trusts a SMART authorization server such as FerroSMART with its `issuer` alone: with no `audience` key the audience is the served version bases under `fhir.base_url`, and such an issuer opens the write routes and not the admin listener. A token's `typ` is `at+jwt` or `application/at+jwt`, `scope` is required, a token whose `sub` is its `client_id` is a backend service, and any other names a user by its `fhirUser`. The authorization server's registry decides which clients reach FerroTERM, through the `aud` it writes. The book gains [Running behind FerroSMART](https://ferroterm.eu/docs/operate/behind-ferrosmart.html).
