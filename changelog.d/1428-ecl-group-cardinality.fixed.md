- ECL attribute group cardinality counts only role groups: a relationship in group 0 is in no group (RF2 §4.2.3), so `[0..0] { [0..0] … }` no longer refuses a concept for its ungrouped attributes. A group whose attributes carry an upper bound or a zero minimum, such as `{ [1..1] 363698007 = << 10200004 }`, now finds every concept with a group that matches, also when the concept has more such rows across its groups.
