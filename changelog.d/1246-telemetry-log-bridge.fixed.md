- The server, the sync worker and the operator console move to ferrohealth-telemetry 0.5, ferrohealth-server 0.9 and ferrohealth-usage-report 0.5, whose subscriber installs its own bridge from the `log` crate in place of `tracing-log`'s `LogTracer`. Under a console filter that lets a target through above its default level, such as `info,ferrohealth_smart=debug`, a dependency's `log::log_enabled!` check no longer drops the next event on its thread, which lost the request line of a request that ran such a check (FerroHEALTH/FerroSYS#388).
