- The standalone ICF releases (`http://hl7.org/fhir/sid/icf`, `2001a` and `2017a`) are served from an operator-supplied `CodeSystem`, with the qualifier grammar of the HL7 terminology ecosystem IG's ICF page. Qualified codes such as `s7300.183` and `e310+3` validate and get their generated displays, report their qualifiers as properties, and sit under their category. The `exists` and `qualified` filters select by what a code states, and an expansion that admits qualified codes is marked unclosed. An `extension-codes` supplement can give a qualified code a display, and the generated display stays valid. FerroTERM ships no ICF content. Over the `fhir.tx.support` releases, the tx-ecosystem `icf` mode passes 79 of 96 cases, where it passed 1.
