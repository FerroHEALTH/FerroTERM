- The issuer keys `kind` (other than `federation`), `preset`, `profile_file` and `scopeless_subjects`, the `[auth.idp]` table, the `persons`, `patients` and `machines` kinds and the profile keys that listed clients or described a vendor's tokens (`first_party_clients`, `delegates`, `audience_delegates`, `issuer_client_is_person`, `act_represents_patient`, `app_only`, `client_claims` and the rest) are removed. A configuration that sets one is refused as an unknown key: drop `kind` and `preset` from a SMART issuer's table and register its clients at the authorization server.
